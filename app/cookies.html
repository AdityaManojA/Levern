Cookies

Last updated 19 October 2026

The short version

Levern sets no cookies of its own and uses no advertising or analytics cookies. It keeps your data in your browser's storage so the app works, which is why there's no cookie banner to click through.

What Levern stores in your browser

These are strictly necessary for the app to work. They stay on your device and aren't used to track you.

NameTypeWhat it's forHow long
taskflow.v1Local storageYour quests, settings, stats and Google Calendar linkUntil you clear it
taskflow-accountLocal storageThe name, email and picture of the Google account you signed in with, so it shows straight awayUntil you sign out
taskflow-swIndexedDBYour reminder schedule, so reminders can show while the app is closedUntil you clear it
taskflow-v*Cache storageA copy of the app's files so it opens offlineReplaced on each update
firebase*IndexedDB / local storageKeeps you signed in (only if you sign in with Google)Until you sign out

Cookies set by Google

Only if you choose to sign in with Google or connect Google Calendar, Google's sign-in window opens on Google's own sites (accounts.google.com and taskflow-dailytracker.firebaseapp.com). Google may set its own cookies there to sign you in and keep that secure. Those are covered by Google's cookie policy.

Your choices

You can clear all of this at any time in your browser's settings for this site, or with Settings → Reset everything in Levern. Blocking storage for this site will stop Levern from saving your quests.

More about what we collect is in the privacy policy.

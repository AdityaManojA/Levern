Privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

The short version

  • Your quests, notes, sketches and settings are stored on your device. We don't have a copy.
  • If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture to show who you are.
  • If you connect Google Calendar, Levern can only see and change the one "Levern" calendar it creates, never your other calendars.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Levern is a to-do app that runs in your browser and can be installed as an app. This policy covers the Levern web app at levern-app.web.app. When we say "we" or "us", we mean the Levern team.

What stays on your device

Everything you put into Levern is saved in your browser's own storage on the device you use: your quests and subtasks, due dates and reminders, notes, sketches and handwriting, your stats and streaks, and your settings (including the name you type in). It is not sent to us. Clearing your browser data for this site, or using Settings → Reset everything, removes it. Settings → Export backup lets you keep a copy as a file.

Because it lives on one device, your quests don't move between your phone and computer yet. If we add syncing between devices, we'll update this policy and ask before anything is uploaded.

Signing in with Google (optional)

You can use Levern without an account. If you choose "Continue with Google", sign-in is handled by Google through Firebase Authentication, a Google service. We receive your Google account's name, email address, profile picture and an account ID. We use them only to show who's signed in and to greet you by name. Google keeps a record of your sign-in in our Firebase project. You can sign out at any time in Settings, and you can ask us to delete your account record (see "Your choices").

Google Calendar (optional)

If you connect Google Calendar, Levern asks Google for one permission: calendar.app.created, which lets it create a calendar called "Levern" and add, change and remove events in that calendar only. It cannot read or change any of your other calendars or events.

Your quests that have a date are copied into that calendar straight from your browser to Google. The access Google gives Levern lasts about an hour and is kept in memory only, never stored. You can disconnect in Settings, and remove Levern's access entirely at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Levern's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don't use that information for ads, don't sell it, and don't let people read it.

Voice input and handwriting

Voice input uses your browser's speech recognition. In Chrome and Edge, the browser sends your recording to Google's or Microsoft's speech service to turn it into text. That happens between your browser and its maker, and Levern only receives the text. Handwriting recognition, where available, runs on your device and nothing is sent anywhere.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are shown by your device. The reminder schedule is kept in your browser so it can show reminders while Levern is closed. Nothing is sent to us.

Services that help run Levern

  • Firebase Hosting (Google) serves the app's files. Like any web host, it handles basic technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages and keep them secure.
  • Firebase Authentication (Google), only if you sign in.
  • Google Identity Services and the Google Calendar API, only if you connect Google Calendar.

Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Our fonts and sounds are stored with the app, so loading them doesn't contact anyone else.

Your choices

  • Use Levern without signing in or connecting a calendar. Everything works without them.
  • Sign out, disconnect Google Calendar, export or reset your data in Settings.
  • Ask us to see or delete the account record Google keeps for your sign-in. Write to the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for Levern.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have legal rights to access, correct, delete or move your data and to object to how it's used. We'll help with any of these requests.

Children

Levern isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects what we collect, we'll tell you in the app first.

# Monetisation plan

*Written 30 October 2026. Levern had 40 testers and 11 regular users at the time.*

With 11 real users, Levern can't make meaningful money yet. Even an unusually good 10% paying rate would mean one paying user. So for now, revenue is about learning whether people will pay at all. The options below are in the order to try them, matched to what Levern already has.

## 1. Sell to teams and offices (highest value per sale)

The tracker already marks **S-002, workplace houses and rooms**, as Premium, priced per office. It's the strongest idea:

- An office of 10–30 people has one person who pays and a real reason to (team motivation). Levern would charge per month instead of chasing individuals.
- It reuses what's already built: leaderboards, seasons, EXP and ranks.
- Try selling it before building it. Ask users and testers: "Would your workplace, class or friend group pay ₹X a month for a private leaderboard?" If 2–3 say yes, build it.

## 2. Levern+ with cosmetic extras

Levern is a game-like to-do app with cats people can pick up and throw, so cosmetics fit naturally and don't hurt the free version:

- New cats or cat outfits (Mochi and Cookie are Levern's most distinctive feature)
- More themes beyond light, dark and Mono
- Profile auras, badges and rank effects (the top-three aura already exists)
- Extra stats, like long-term trends and history export

Charge a small monthly price or a one-time unlock. Keep the core to-do features free.

## 3. A one-time "Founding supporter" pack (fastest to set up)

Offer a lifetime pack to early users, with a permanent founder badge (building on the OG badge), an exclusive cat colour and their name on a supporters list. Early users tend to buy these out of goodwill, and it shows quickly whether anyone will pay. It could go live within a week.

## 4. Tip jar

Add a "Buy Mochi a treat" button in Settings, linking to Ko-fi, Buy Me a Coffee or a Razorpay page. It takes almost no work but won't bring in much.

## What to skip

- **Ads:** a calm to-do app with this few users earns close to nothing from ads and loses its appeal.
- **Locking core features** like reminders, sync or calendar behind payment: it would lose users Levern can't afford to lose.

## Practical notes

- **Taking payments on the free Firebase plan:** the project can't run payment-checking server code yet. Start with a payment link (Razorpay, Lemon Squeezy or Stripe) and unlock purchases by hand. That's fine at this size; automate later.
- **No app store fees:** Levern is a web app, not a store app, so it keeps the share Apple or Google would take.
- **Growth matters more right now:** getting from 11 to 100+ users matters more than which pricing is chosen. Ask the 29 testers who didn't become regular users why.

## Recommendation

Launch the founding-supporter pack now (#3) and, in the same message, ask whether people's teams would pay for private leaderboards (#1). The founder pack shows whether anyone pays; the team idea is where real money could come from.
